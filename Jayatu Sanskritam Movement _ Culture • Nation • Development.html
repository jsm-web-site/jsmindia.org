Who we are

About JSM

The Jayatu Sanskritam Movement (JSM) is an Indian movement based in Kolkata, West Bengal. We believe India’s progress should grow from its own civilisation, languages and traditions, while building modern institutions, industry and strong governance.

JSM is at an early stage. We are building a dependable team of genuine members, beginning in West Bengal, and preparing to seek registration as a political party with the Election Commission of India.

We would rather grow honestly and steadily than make claims we cannot back up.

What we stand for

Our Vision

Culture

Protect and promote India’s heritage, regional traditions, Sanskrit and Indian languages, and a confident civilisational identity, including Bengal’s own proud culture.

Nation

A secure, united and self-respecting India, with strong national security and national integration across regions and communities.

Development

Industrial growth, economic self-reliance, better education and efficient institutions, so that development reaches ordinary people.

“What Bengal thinks today, India thinks tomorrow.”
