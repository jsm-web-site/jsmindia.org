Preamble

Jayatu Sanskritam Movement (hereinafter referred to as “JSM”) is an organised political movement established with the objective of contributing to the political, social, cultural and economic development of India.

The Movement recognises India’s civilisational heritage, its cultural diversity, its historical continuity and its constitutional democratic framework.

The Movement seeks to promote national development, cultural preservation, social advancement, economic opportunity, good governance, institutional accountability and national unity.

The Movement shall function as a democratic political organisation, committed to constitutional methods, public participation, organisational discipline and the rule of law.

The Movement shall initially concentrate its organisational activities in the State of West Bengal, while retaining the objective of establishing a nationwide organisational presence.

The following Constitution shall govern the organisation, administration, membership, leadership, activities and internal democratic processes of Jayatu Sanskritam Movement.

I

Article I
Name, Identity and Headquarters

1.1

Name

The name of the organisation shall be Jayatu Sanskritam Movement, hereinafter abbreviated as JSM.

1.2

Nature

JSM shall function as a political movement and, following registration under applicable law, as a political party.

1.3

Headquarters

The principal headquarters of JSM shall be situated in West Bengal, India, at an address formally approved by the National Executive Committee.

1.4

National Jurisdiction

The organisation shall have jurisdiction throughout the territory of India and may establish organisational units in any State or Union Territory.

1.5

Official Identity

The official motto of JSM shall be: CULTURE • NATION • DEVELOPMENT. The official emblem, flag, colours and other identifying symbols shall be determined by the National Executive Committee, subject to applicable law.

II

Article II
Objectives

Objectives of JSM shall be:

  1. To promote the unity, sovereignty and integrity of India.
  2. To strengthen India’s constitutional democracy and parliamentary institutions.
  3. To preserve, promote and develop India’s civilisational, linguistic, historical and cultural heritage.
  4. To encourage the preservation and development of Sanskrit and other Indian languages.
  5. To promote the economic development of India through productive industry, infrastructure, scientific advancement, technological innovation and employment generation.
  6. To encourage social welfare, education, healthcare and public development.
  7. To promote national self-reliance, domestic production, indigenous enterprise and economic resilience.
  8. To strengthen public administration, institutional accountability and good governance.
  9. To promote the development of West Bengal and its people, while supporting the broader development of the Indian Union.
  10. To encourage the participation of youth, women, students, workers, professionals and other sections of society in public life.
  11. To contest elections and participate in democratic political processes in accordance with applicable laws.
  12. To cooperate with other political organisations and democratic institutions where such cooperation is consistent with the Movement’s objectives.
  13. To promote peace, public order, national integration and constitutional methods of political activity.
III

Article III
Constitutional Commitment

3.1

In accordance with Section 29A(5) of the Representation of the People Act, 1951, the following provision shall be binding upon the organisation:

Constitutional declaration. “Jayatu Sanskritam Movement shall bear true faith and allegiance to the Constitution of India as by law established, and to the principles of socialism, secularism and democracy and would uphold the sovereignty, unity and integrity of India.”
3.2

JSM shall conduct its activities in accordance with the Constitution of India and the laws in force.

3.3

No organisational decision, resolution or activity shall override this constitutional commitment.

IV

Article IV
Membership

4.1

Eligibility

Membership shall be open to every Indian citizen who: (a) has attained eighteen years of age; (b) accepts the Constitution, objectives and rules of JSM; (c) is not otherwise disqualified under applicable law or valid organisational rules; and (d) agrees to uphold the constitutional and democratic principles of the Movement. Membership shall not be denied on grounds of caste, religion, language, sex, place of birth or community.

4.2

Categories of Membership

  • Ordinary Member.
  • Active Member.
  • Office-Bearer.
  • Life Member, if introduced by the National Executive Committee.
4.3

Ordinary Membership

  • Participate in authorised organisational activities.
  • Attend general membership meetings.
  • Express opinions on organisational matters.
  • Apply for elevation to Active Membership.
  • Participate in internal democratic processes, subject to applicable eligibility requirements.
4.4

Active Membership

Active membership shall be granted to members who demonstrate sustained participation in organisational activities. Active members shall be eligible for organisational responsibilities and internal elections, subject to the relevant rules.

4.5

Membership Register

The organisation shall maintain an official membership register containing relevant membership particulars.

4.6

Membership Fees

Membership fees, if applicable, shall be determined by the National Executive Committee and approved by the National Council.

4.7

Resignation

Any member may resign by submitting written notice to the appropriate organisational authority.

4.8

Termination

Membership may be terminated for serious violations of the Constitution, subject to disciplinary proceedings and the right to appeal.

V

Article V
Organisational Structure

5.1

JSM shall maintain the following organisational hierarchy:

Hierarchy:

  1. National General Council.
  2. National Executive Committee.
  3. National President.
  4. National Secretariat.
  5. State Executive Committees.
  6. District Executive Committees.
  7. Sub-Divisional and Block Committees.
  8. Municipal and Ward Committees.
  9. Primary and Local Units.
5.2

The organisational hierarchy shall operate under this Constitution and the rules adopted pursuant to it.

VI

Article VI
National General Council

6.1

Composition

The National General Council shall be the highest deliberative and constitutional authority of JSM. It shall comprise the National President; members of the National Executive Committee; State Presidents; elected representatives of State organisational units; and other elected or nominated representatives as prescribed by organisational regulations.

6.2

Powers

  • Approve and amend the Constitution.
  • Establish the general political and organisational direction of JSM.
  • Elect the National President and other elected national office-bearers.
  • Approve major organisational policies.
  • Review the performance of the National Executive Committee.
  • Approve annual organisational reports.
  • Consider matters of national importance.
  • Approve mergers, dissolution or other fundamental changes, subject to this Constitution and applicable law.
6.3

Meetings

The National General Council shall ordinarily meet at least once every year. A special meeting may be convened by the National President or upon a written request from at least one-third of its members.

6.4

Quorum

One-third of the total membership of the National General Council shall constitute the quorum.

VII

Article VII
National Executive Committee

7.1

Composition

  • National President.
  • Vice-Presidents.
  • National General Secretary.
  • National Secretaries.
  • National Treasurer.
  • National Organising Secretary.
  • National Publicity and Communications Secretary.
  • Other office-bearers approved under organisational regulations.
7.2

Powers and Functions

  • Implement decisions of the National General Council.
  • Supervise national organisational administration.
  • Coordinate State Executive Committees.
  • Approve organisational programmes and campaigns.
  • Establish departments, cells and committees.
  • Manage authorised organisational expenditure.
  • Coordinate political and electoral activities.
  • Issue administrative regulations consistent with this Constitution.
  • Prepare annual reports and financial statements.
7.3

Meetings

The NEC shall meet at least once every three months. Emergency meetings may be convened whenever necessary.

7.4

Decision-Making

Decisions shall ordinarily be taken by majority vote. In the event of a tie, the presiding officer shall have a casting vote. No office-bearer shall possess an absolute or permanent veto over the organisation’s constitutional decision-making.

VIII

Article VIII
National President

8.1

Position

The National President shall be the principal elected political and organisational leader of JSM.

8.2

Responsibilities

  • Provide political and organisational leadership.
  • Preside over National General Council and NEC meetings.
  • Represent JSM in official political and organisational engagements.
  • Supervise the implementation of organisational policies.
  • Coordinate the work of national office-bearers.
  • Present organisational reports to the National General Council.
  • Ensure compliance with this Constitution.
  • Represent the organisation before public authorities, subject to applicable authorisations.
8.3

Election

The National President shall be elected by the National General Council through a democratic voting process.

8.4

Term

The term of the National President shall be three years. A President may be re-elected, subject to the provisions of this Constitution.

8.5

Vacancy

In the event of resignation, death, removal or permanent incapacity, the senior-most Vice-President shall serve as Acting President until an election is conducted. An election to fill the vacancy shall ordinarily take place within six months.

8.6

Removal

The National President may be removed through a no-confidence resolution supported by at least two-thirds of the total membership of the National General Council, following due notice and an opportunity to respond.

IX

Article IX
National Secretariat

9.1

Establishment

A National Secretariat shall function as the central administrative office of JSM.

9.2

National General Secretary

  • Administrative coordination.
  • Maintaining official organisational records.
  • Issuing meeting notices and minutes.
  • Maintaining membership and office-bearer records.
  • Coordinating communication between national and State units.
  • Monitoring implementation of NEC decisions.
  • Maintaining official correspondence.
9.3

National Treasurer

  • Maintain financial records.
  • Supervise authorised receipts and expenditure.
  • Prepare annual financial statements.
  • Coordinate statutory financial reporting.
  • Maintain records of contributions and donations.
  • Ensure proper auditing of organisational accounts.
9.4

National Organising Secretary

  • Develop organisational expansion plans.
  • Coordinate membership drives.
  • Supervise organisational development at State and district levels.
  • Maintain organisational performance reports.
  • Assist in establishing new organisational units.
X

Article X
State Organisations

10.1

Establishment

A State Executive Committee may be established in any State or Union Territory where JSM has sufficient organisational presence.

10.2

West Bengal

West Bengal shall serve as the initial principal organisational base of JSM. The West Bengal State Executive Committee shall coordinate the Movement’s activities throughout the State.

10.3

Composition

  • State President.
  • State Vice-Presidents.
  • State General Secretary.
  • State Secretaries.
  • State Treasurer.
  • State Organising Secretary.
  • Other authorised office-bearers.
10.4

Powers

  • Implement national policies within their jurisdiction.
  • Develop State-level political and organisational programmes.
  • Coordinate district and local units.
  • Maintain State membership records.
  • Prepare State-level reports.
  • Conduct authorised political activities.
  • Recommend candidates for electoral consideration.
  • Submit periodic reports to the NEC.
10.5

Accountability

State units shall remain constitutionally subordinate to the National Executive Committee while retaining authority over ordinary State-level administration.

XI

Article XI
District and Local Organisations

11.1

JSM may establish the following subordinate organisational units: District Committees; Sub-Divisional Committees; Block Committees; Municipal Committees; Ward Committees; and Primary or Local Committees.

11.2

Each unit shall have a President, Secretary, Treasurer where required, and other office-bearers as prescribed by regulations.

11.3

Functions

  • Recruit and maintain members.
  • Conduct local organisational activities.
  • Communicate public concerns to higher authorities.
  • Implement approved programmes.
  • Submit regular activity reports.
  • Maintain records of meetings and expenditure.
11.4

No subordinate unit may adopt a resolution inconsistent with this Constitution or a lawful decision of a superior organisational authority.

XII

Article XII
Organisational Departments and Wings

12.1

Departments

  • Department of Organisation.
  • Department of Political Affairs.
  • Department of Policy and Research.
  • Department of Finance and Administration.
  • Department of Public Relations and Communications.
  • Department of Legal Affairs.
  • Department of Electoral Affairs.
  • Department of Training and Development.
  • Department of Digital Operations.
12.2

Wings

  • Youth Wing.
  • Student Wing.
  • Women’s Wing.
  • Workers’ Wing.
  • Cultural and Heritage Wing.
  • Professional and Intellectual Wing.
12.3

The NEC may establish permanent or temporary departments and affiliated or internal wings. Each shall operate under rules approved by the NEC. No wing shall independently exercise powers reserved for the National Executive Committee or National General Council.

XIII

Article XIII
Internal Organisational Elections

13.1

Democratic Elections

All principal organisational office-bearers shall be elected through democratic internal procedures.

13.2

Election Periodicity

Organisational elections shall ordinarily take place every three years.

13.3

Election Authority

An independent Internal Election Committee shall supervise national organisational elections. Its members shall not contest the elections they supervise.

13.4

Electoral Procedure

Elections shall be conducted through secret ballot or another secure, transparent voting procedure approved under election regulations.

13.5

Eligibility

Candidates must satisfy membership, organisational standing and other reasonable eligibility requirements established by the NEC.

13.6

Election Records

The organisation shall preserve election notices, candidate nominations, voter lists, results and other relevant records.

13.7

Accountability

No office-bearer shall continue indefinitely without periodic organisational elections.

XIV

Article XIV
Finance and Accounts

14.1

Sources of Funds

  • Membership subscriptions.
  • Voluntary contributions.
  • Donations.
  • Permitted fundraising activities.
  • Other lawful sources.
14.2

Banking

All organisational funds shall be maintained through authorised bank accounts in the organisation’s name.

14.3

Financial Authority

The NEC shall establish financial controls, including authorised signatories and expenditure approval procedures.

14.4

Financial Year

The financial year shall ordinarily run from 1 April to 31 March.

14.5

Accounts

Proper books of accounts shall be maintained at national and State levels, as applicable.

14.6

Audit

The accounts of JSM shall be audited annually by a qualified independent auditor.

14.7

Financial Reporting

Annual financial statements and reports shall be presented to the National General Council.

14.8

Legal Compliance

All contributions, donations, expenditure, disclosures and financial reports shall comply with applicable election and financial laws.

XV

Article XV
Discipline and Internal Accountability

15.1

Disciplinary Authority

A National Disciplinary Committee shall be established by the NEC.

15.2

Grounds for Disciplinary Action

  • Serious violations of this Constitution.
  • Financial misconduct.
  • Misuse of organisational authority.
  • Deliberate falsification of organisational records.
  • Serious acts of indiscipline.
  • Unauthorised representation of JSM.
  • Conduct materially damaging to the organisation.
15.3

Disciplinary Measures

  • Written warning.
  • Formal censure.
  • Temporary suspension.
  • Removal from organisational office.
  • Termination of membership.
15.4

Due Process

No member shall be subjected to major disciplinary punishment without receiving written notice of the allegations and a reasonable opportunity to respond.

15.5

Appeals

An affected member may appeal to an independent National Appeals Committee within thirty days of receiving the disciplinary decision. The appeals authority shall not consist of persons who made the original disciplinary decision.

XVI

Article XVI
Candidate Selection and Electoral Participation

16.1

Electoral Participation

JSM may contest elections to Parliament, State Legislatures, local authorities and other elected institutions in accordance with applicable law.

16.2

Candidate Selection

  • Public standing.
  • Organisational contribution.
  • Integrity and eligibility.
  • Electoral suitability.
  • Commitment to the Constitution and objectives of JSM.
16.3

State Recommendations

State Executive Committees may recommend candidates to the NEC.

16.4

Final Approval

Final organisational approval of candidates shall rest with the competent national or State authority as determined by electoral regulations and applicable law.

16.5

Alliances

JSM may enter into electoral or political alliances with other political organisations through a resolution of the NEC. Major alliance decisions may be referred to the National General Council for approval.

XVII

Article XVII
Official Communication and Representation

17.1

Only authorised office-bearers and representatives may issue official statements on behalf of JSM.

17.2

The National President, National General Secretary and authorised Publicity and Communications Secretary shall be principal national spokesperson authorities.

17.3

State units may issue statements concerning State matters within their jurisdiction, subject to organisational policy.

17.4

All official communications shall reflect the Constitution and authorised policies of the Movement.

XVIII

Article XVIII
Constitutional Amendments

18.1

Amendment Authority

This Constitution may be amended by the National General Council.

18.2

Proposal

Amendments may be proposed by the National Executive Committee or at least one-third of the National General Council.

18.3

Notice

Proposed amendments shall be circulated to members of the National General Council at least thirty days before the meeting at which they are considered.

18.4

Approval

An amendment shall require the support of at least two-thirds of members present and voting, provided the meeting satisfies the prescribed quorum.

18.5

Legal Compliance

No amendment shall violate the constitutional commitment contained in Article III or applicable Indian law. Any amendment required to be reported to the Election Commission or another authority shall be duly communicated in accordance with applicable requirements.

XIX

Article XIX
Interpretation of the Constitution

19.1

The NEC shall be responsible for the ordinary interpretation and administration of this Constitution.

19.2

Any major constitutional dispute may be referred to the National General Council.

19.3

The NEC’s interpretation shall remain subject to review by the National General Council.

19.4

No interpretation may override an express provision of this Constitution.

XX

Article XX
Merger, Dissolution and Succession

20.1

Merger

JSM may merge with another political organisation through a resolution supported by at least two-thirds of the total membership of the National General Council, subject to applicable law.

20.2

Dissolution

The organisation may be dissolved only through a special resolution supported by at least three-fourths of the total membership of the National General Council.

20.3

Settlement of Liabilities

In the event of dissolution, all liabilities shall be settled in accordance with applicable law. Any remaining assets shall be dealt with in accordance with the law and the dissolution resolution. They shall not be distributed among individual members.

XXI

Article XXI
General Provisions

21.1

Rules and Regulations

The NEC may adopt additional rules and regulations necessary for the functioning of JSM, provided they do not contradict this Constitution.

21.2

Organisational Records

  • Membership registers.
  • Minutes of meetings.
  • Election records.
  • Financial statements.
  • Office-bearer registers.
  • Organisational reports.
  • Official correspondence.
21.3

Official Language

JSM may conduct its activities in Bengali, Hindi, English, Sanskrit and other Indian languages as appropriate.

21.4

Supremacy

This Constitution shall be the supreme internal governing document of JSM. All office-bearers, departments, committees and organisational units shall be bound by its provisions.

XXII

Article XXII
Commencement and Adoption

22.1

This Constitution shall come into force upon its adoption by the founding members of Jayatu Sanskritam Movement at a duly convened organisational meeting.

22.2

The founding members shall record their approval through a formal resolution and authenticate the adopted document.

22.3

Any subsequent amendments shall be made in accordance with Article XVIII.
